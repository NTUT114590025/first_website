Taiwan NO.1!!!

Where mountain meets modern life.

Taiwan is an island full of captivating endless surprises. One moment, you are walking through vibrant and historic night markets. A short drive away, you can immerse yourself in serene mountain tea plantations and ancient temples hidden among high rises.

Rich heritage and culinary wonders.

From the iconic skyline dominated by Taipei 101 to the picturesque alleyways of Jiufen, Taiwan seamlessly blends traditional culture with forward-looking innovation. Experience world-class hospitality, rich history, and unforgettable street food.
